10. Important Data-Cleaning Decision

We noticed that some mandi names and districts appear geographically unusual.

For example:

Hyderabad Mandi → Ludhiana
Kochi Mandi → Amritsar

We did not manually correct these.

Why?

Because the dataset is synthetic/educational and the goal is to rescue and standardize the supplied data, not invent replacements based on our assumptions.

This is an important point we can later mention in the README/report:

Only deterministic formatting and normalization corrections were applied. Potentially anomalous business values were retained unless they could be validated from another source within the dataset.

That's a strong data-engineering practice.
